Vietnamese Pho

Pho is my favorite food because it is delicious, warm, and healthy. It is a traditional Vietnamese noodle soup made with broth, rice noodles, meat, and fresh herbs.

I love eating pho with my family. The delicious broth and fresh ingredients make this dish special.
