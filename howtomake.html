Cooking Steps

  1. Prepare the onion, ginger, and spices.
  2. Simmer the broth until it becomes fragrant.
  3. Cook the rice noodles according to the instructions.
  4. Put the noodles and meat into a bowl.
  5. Pour the hot broth over the noodles.
  6. Add green onions, cilantro, basil, and lime.
  7. Serve hot and enjoy your pho.
