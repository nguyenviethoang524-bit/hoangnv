Ingredients

Here are the main ingredients for making Vietnamese pho.

  • Rice noodles
  • Beef or chicken
  • Beef or chicken broth
  • Onion and ginger
  • Star anise and cinnamon
  • Fish sauce
  • Green onions and cilantro
  • Bean sprouts and fresh basil
  • Lime
